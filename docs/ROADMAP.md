# Public roadmap

OpenTabletDisplay's detailed engineering roadmap is maintained privately until work is ready to ship.

Public priorities are intentionally high level:

- make installation, updates, reconnects, and uninstall behavior more reliable;
- broaden real-device compatibility and validation;
- improve diagnostics and user-facing failure reporting;
- strengthen release trust and distribution; and
- keep the wired second-display experience simple and low-friction.

Specific unreleased architecture, implementation plans, experiments, and release candidates are not published here ahead of validation.
